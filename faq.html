What is a weekend calendar?

A calendar designed around Saturday and Sunday. Each weekend gets a large card, so there is real room for family plans, trips and little adventures.

How many weekends does 2027 have?

Fifty-two full weekends, from 2 January to 26 December.

What size is it?

A3, portrait, with Wire-O binding. Designed to hang on a wall.
