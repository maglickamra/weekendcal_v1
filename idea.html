The idea

Life happens
on the weekend.

Why a weekend calendar?

Why do weekends get
so little space?

Our calendars are full of weekdays. Meetings. Work. School. Appointments. Training. Deadlines. We squeeze our lives into tiny boxes and fill every available space with things we have to do.

But the weekend?
That's when we live.

Family trips. Birthdays. Friends. Sports. Long breakfasts. Slow mornings. New places. Little adventures.

So why do weekends get the smallest space in our calendars? We thought they deserved more. So we made a calendar that gives them more.
