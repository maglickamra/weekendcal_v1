About, brand philosophy

Make room for
those two days.

We spend five days getting things done. Then we get two days to remember why we're doing it.

As a mother of two daughters, I used to feel the pressure every single weekend to make the most of our precious family time.

I would spend the week thinking: what are we going to do this weekend? Where should we go? What can we discover together? And then, somehow, the weekend would pass. A bit of laundry. A trip to the supermarket. A lazy morning at home. Maybe a playground. And suddenly it was Monday again.

Then one day, I looked back at the last three weekends and realised something that genuinely frustrated me: six precious free days had passed, and we hadn't really done anything new.

Because the truth is, weekends are not unlimited. We only get so many of them while our children are little. So I thought: not anymore.

What if there was a calendar designed not around weekdays, but around the days we actually look forward to? A calendar that inspires you to get out, explore, try something new, make plans, and turn ordinary weekends into memories.

That's how the Weekend Calendar was born.

Make room for it.
